About

Tim holds an Executive MBA from W. P. Carey (ASU) and a B.S. in Professional Aeronautics from Embry-Riddle. He currently serves as an Adjunct Professor of Leadership at Northern Arizona University and is pursuing a PhD in Industrial-Organizational Psychology at Grand Canyon University.

Training

PROPRIETARY FRAMEWORKS.

HPE Systems has developed three proprietary frameworks applied across training programs, consulting engagements, and leadership development. These are not off-the-shelf methodologies — they are built from decades of practice in environments where performance failures have real consequences.

HAPPI
Human Accountability & Performance Process Integration

A structured model for aligning individual performance behaviors with organizational quality systems. Bridges the gap between human factors and process compliance in high-consequence environments.

SAUL-T
Situational Awareness & Unified Leadership Transformation

A leadership framework developed for organizations navigating complexity and change. Builds adaptive decision-making capacity at every level of an organization, from front-line supervisors to senior executives.

Step Zero
Pre-Decision Architecture for Organizational Initiatives

A decision-making framework that clarifies intent, alignment, and readiness before committing organizational resources. Prevents costly misalignments between strategic goals and operational execution.

Philosophy

HUMAN CAPITAL
IS THE VARIABLE.

Investment research now quantifies workforce quality as a predictor of market performance. HPE Systems builds the systems that move that number.

A new body of research is making the case to institutional investors that how organizations develop, engage, and deploy their people is one of the most reliable signals of long-term performance. The data is catching up to what practitioners already know.

HPE Systems exists at the implementation layer of that thesis. Quality management systems, auditor development, leadership frameworks, and process improvement are not compliance exercises — they are the mechanisms through which human performance becomes organizational performance.

When the measurement catches up to the mission, the organizations that built their systems right will already be there.

01
Systems Before Symptoms

Nonconformances, audit findings, and performance gaps are symptoms. HPE Systems traces them to the system conditions that produce them — and fixes those.

02
Competence Over Compliance

A document is not evidence of capability. Training that produces auditors who can find real systemic issues — not just fill out checklists — is the standard HPE Systems builds toward.

03
Performance Is Measurable

The HPE Systems frameworks — HAPPI, SAUL-T, and Step Zero — exist because human performance can be designed, measured, and improved. Not hoped for.

04
Outcomes, Not Outputs

Training is measured by what happens after — did the auditor find nonconformances? Did leadership behavior change? Did the organization improve? Those are the metrics that matter.
